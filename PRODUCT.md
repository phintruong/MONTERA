# Product

register: product

## Purpose
An interactive 3D assembly guide for IKEA flat-pack furniture (v1: LACK side table). It replaces the wordless paper manual with a step player someone can drive hands-free. It succeeds if people flip fewer panels, grab the right screw, and get stuck less.

## Users
- Primary: someone building furniture alone at home, kneeling on the floor, phone or laptop propped against the box about 1 m away, hands full of parts. Glances, doesn't read.
- Secondary: a helper looking at the same screen.

## Scene
Daytime living room, warm lamp or window light, person on the floor glancing up at a phone leaning on cardboard. Forces: light theme, very high contrast, huge step numbers, big targets, almost no chrome.

## Brand
Speaks IKEA: the IKEA.com visual language (yellow #FFDB00, blue #0058A3, ink #111111 on warm canvas #FFFEFB, Noto IKEA / Noto Sans at 400 and 700) and the paper manual's line-drawing look (white parts, black outlines, a big step numeral).

## Principles
- The 3D view is the hero; the UI gets out of its way.
- One instruction at a time. Every word earns its place.
- Colour means something: blue = act on this, yellow = watch out / finished face.
- Readable from 1 m: nothing important below 16 px.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS dashboards, card stacks, glassmorphism, drop shadows, emoji icons.
